# Measurement assertions: domain contract

Measurement assertions preserve exact entered values and how they were obtained.
The backend increment is default-disabled and separate from any inspector mount.
It does not provision a measurement image, alter geometry, or verify a house.

## Values and targets

Keep decimalToken, unit and quantity kind separate from display rounding. Validate
bounded decimal syntax and compare supported metric quantities exactly. Preserve
the original token, including meaningful entered formatting; conversions and
rounded labels never replace it. Inventory derived model polygon area and spans
as approximate observations, without inventing dimensions or stable wall IDs.

Assertions address stable semantic targets and exact source revision, source hash,
artifact identity/hash and publication association. Building, level, occurrence,
plan and source-object identities may be bounded producer-owned strings; a room
or native record may have a UUID. Artifact-local node, mesh and primitive indices
are correspondence details, not persistent physical targets. Presentation ordinals
must never identify a correction. Reordered topology cannot silently retarget it.

Lineage represents both artifact-pinned provisional publications and fully native
custody. Missing Geometry/SceneAssociation receipts remain unknown; historical
place bindings cannot manufacture them. Publication replacement retains old
assertions and requires reviewed pairwise association. A shared native room only
identifies association candidates, not applicability to a new occurrence or part.

## Acquisition, evidence and support

Methods distinguish guessed, estimated, directly measured, photo-fitted, derived,
imported, legacy user-reported method-unspecified, and explicit user-reported
measured acquisition. Image-informed estimated geometry stays estimated. A photo
alone proves neither an accepted camera fit nor direct physical measurement.

The ordinary personal path requires explicit personally-measured attestation;
typing a number does not imply measurement. It needs no photo or certificate.
Its presentation meaning is Measured · user-reported. Original authenticated
transaction ownership supplies actor and recording time; recording time is not
physical measurement time. Corroboration stays unestablished, uncertainty unknown,
and no precision margin is invented. Corrections retain their original attribution;
derived inputs retain personal origin and every inherited support component.

Reviewed direct measurement retains its native Evidence meaning. A corroborated
photo fit remains photo-fitted. Acquisition method, support/uncertainty, evidence
roles/revisions, disputes, evidence retirement, assertion retirement and model
reconciliation are independent. There is no confidence ordering that promotes
methods or erases limitations. Multiple evidence sources may support an assertion.
Historical contextual evidence is distinct from withdrawn/superseded support.

## Immutable revisions, contradictions and pending work

Corrections preserve exact prior values, provenance and supersession lineage.
Unresolved contradictions survive successor revisions until an explicit reviewed
resolution identifies what it resolves. Active same-target/property unequal exact
quantities require review even without an explicit contradiction link; equivalent
units compare exactly. Equal area does not establish equal shape or correspondence.
Ambiguous/inconsistent claims require review, never silent geometric distortion.

Saving an assertion never closes model work. Pending work binds each exact
assertion version and artifact association. Closure would require independent
native correspondence for that exact assertion revision/artifact and qualification
of every retained support component, evidence role/revision and reviewed dispute
disposition. Unresolved disputes, unavailable support qualification, unknown
custody/registration or stale verification cannot yield closure eligibility.
No closure endpoint or geometry writer is supplied by this increment.

## Boundaries and qualification

The online contract, same-store persistence, original-authority fences and profile
compatibility are specified in the adjacent contract documents. Existing
reconciliationPayload remains native binding remapping and is not repurposed.
The eventual ordinary interface may expose measurement, provenance, edit and a
lightweight pending indicator; no prominent ledger/history screen is required.
Area is the first online increment; lengths, widths, heights and perimeter remain
follow-on work. No area entry establishes coverage of an entire logical room.

Synthetic domain cases and selected native compositions have historical bounded
qualification on their recorded source trees. Source formatting and composition
produce a new candidate: prior results are not a fresh qualification of it. The
verification guide records exact selectors and held lanes. No installed, browser,
physical model, deployment or security acceptance follows from those checks.
